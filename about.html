About SAKHI

A platform created to support, empower and spread awareness about women's safety.

🌸

Our Mission

To make safety information and emergency support easily accessible to women through one platform.

💜

Our Vision

A world where every woman feels confident, informed, independent and safe.

🤝

Our Purpose

To connect women with emergency services, legal resources and useful safety guidance.

What SAKHI Offers

• Emergency helpline information

• Police and hospital assistance resources

• Personal safety tools

• Trusted contact support

• Women's rights and legal awareness

• Cyber safety information
